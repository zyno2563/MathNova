Privacy Policy

MathNova V1 has no accounts, no cookies and no analytics. This page says exactly what happens to what you type.

Last updated 20 September 2026

The short version

  • We do not ask who you are, and there is nothing to sign up for.
  • What you type is sent to the server, calculated, and returned. It is not written to a database.
  • If you use the AI Assistant, your question goes to a third-party model provider. That is the one place your text leaves this service.
  • No cookies, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking across sites.

What is processed

Your mathematical input
Expressions, coefficients and matrices are sent to the MathNova API, computed, and returned in the response. They are held in memory for the life of the request and are not stored afterwards.
Assistant conversations
Your question, recent turns of the same conversation, and the name of the module you are viewing are sent to the configured AI provider. The conversation lives in the page and is gone when you close the tab.
Server logs
The web server records ordinary operational entries: the time, the path requested, the status code. These are for running the service and diagnosing faults.
Browser storage
Your colour-theme choice, kept in this browser’s local storage. It never leaves your device and is not a cookie. Clear it from Settings.

The AI Assistant and third parties

When the assistant is enabled, your question is sent to an external model provider so it can be answered. What that provider does with it is governed by their privacy policy and terms, not ours, and may include retention for their own purposes. We do not control that.

Do not put anything confidential into the assistant. No personal data, no identifying details, no commercially sensitive or unpublished work. If you would not paste it into a public chat window, do not paste it here.

The Solver does not use the AI provider at all. If you want your input to stay within MathNova, use the Solver rather than the assistant.

Other third parties

Mathematical typesetting (KaTeX) is loaded from a public CDN, so your browser makes a request to that CDN, which will see your IP address as any web request does. No MathNova data is sent with it.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking scripts of any kind.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of data — there is no user data to sell.
  • No profiling, and no automated decisions about you.

Children

MathNova is intended for students and professionals studying or working in engineering mathematics. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Rights over personal data — access, correction, erasure — apply to personal data that is held. MathNova holds none: there is no account, no profile and no stored history, so there is nothing to retrieve or delete. Your theme preference is in your own browser and yours to clear at any time.

For anything sent to the AI provider, that provider is the one holding it, and their policy governs it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. A change that materially affects what happens to your input will be described here rather than made quietly.
