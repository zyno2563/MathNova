 ∑

Fourier series

Coefficients a₀, aₙ, bₙ, the partial-sum reconstruction, and the error at a sample point.

Why the answers are checked

A symbolic result that looks plausible and is wrong is worse than no result. So every engine verifies its own work by a route that does not reuse the step being tested: a solved ODE is substituted back into the original operator and the residual must be zero, an inverse transform is transformed forward again, a linear system is re-multiplied, a derivative is re-derived implicitly.

Where a check passes, the result says so. Where an engine cannot verify something, it tells you rather than quietly presenting an unchecked answer. That discipline is the point of the project — it is also how several real errors were found and fixed during development.
