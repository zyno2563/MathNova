AI Mathematics Assistant

Ask in plain English. The assistant does not do the algebra itself — it calls the same verified MathNova engines the Solver uses, then explains what came back. The tools it ran are shown under each answer.

Check before you rely on it. The calculations come from verified engines, but the explanation around them is generated by a language model and can be wrong, incomplete, or confidently mistaken about what you asked. It is not a substitute for your own working — see the AI & Mathematical Disclaimer.
