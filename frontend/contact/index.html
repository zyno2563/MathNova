Reporting a wrong answer

This is the most valuable report we can receive, and the easiest to act on if you include the input. Please send:

  1. The module and method — for example “Transforms → inverse Z-transform”.
  2. The exact input you entered, copied rather than retyped.
  3. What MathNova returned, and what you expected instead.
  4. Your working, if you have it — it usually shows immediately where the two diverge.

Every engine checks its own results, so a wrong answer that still reports itself as verified points at a real defect in the verification and is treated as a priority.

Reporting a problem with the site

For anything that is not a mathematical error — a page that will not load, a control that does nothing, layout that breaks on your screen — please include:

  • The page address.
  • Your browser and version, and whether you are on a phone, tablet or desktop.
  • What you did, what happened, and what you expected.
  • A screenshot, if it is a visual problem.

The AI Assistant

If the assistant gives a bad answer, please include the question you asked and the reply you got. Note whether any tool chips appeared under the answer: they show which engines were called, and their absence usually means the model answered from its own knowledge instead of calling one — which is a bug worth reporting.

What we cannot help with

We cannot solve your coursework or assessed problems for you, confirm whether an answer will be accepted by your institution, or provide professional engineering advice. MathNova is a calculation tool and a study aid, and the AI & Mathematical Disclaimer sets out what that does and does not mean.

Response times

MathNova is a small project without a support team, so replies are on a best-effort basis and there is no guaranteed response time. Clear reports with the input included are the ones that get fixed fastest.
